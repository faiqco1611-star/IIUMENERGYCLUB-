About Us

IIUM Energy Club is a student-led platform that connects university students with the energy field through programmes, projects, and industry engagement.

We strive to bridge students with the energy industry while fostering awareness, innovation, and teamwork within the university community.

"We are living in the era of green energy, sustainability, and renewable energy."

Our Vision

To be the leading platform in IIUM that exposes students to the energy industry, fosters a deep understanding of Malaysia's energy sector, and inspires enthusiasm for contributing to the field.

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge and insights into the energy sector, raise awareness about sustainable energy solutions, and create opportunities for them to excel in the industry.
